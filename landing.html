How it works

Up and running in minutes.

Install, join your meeting, and Meeto handles the rest.

1

Install & configure

Download the Windows installer. Point Meeto at your microphone and optionally add your Claude API key for AI card generation.

2

Load your context

Drop in PDFs, briefs, or reference docs. Meeto indexes them locally so the AI knows your products, clients, and talking points.

3

Meet — Meeto takes notes

Hit Record. Watch the transcript roll in, insight cards appear in real time, and action items capture themselves as they're spoken.

Privacy-first by design

Your conversations
stay yours.

Most AI meeting tools ship your audio and transcripts to data centers you've never heard of. Meeto does the opposite.

  • 🔒 Whisper AI transcription runs entirely on-device — audio is never streamed or stored off-machine.
  • 💾 All transcripts, cards, and tasks are saved in a local SQLite database. No sync, no backup service, no third-party storage.
  • 🔑 Claude API is optional and opt-in. You provide your own API key. Transcripts sent to Claude contain only what you choose.
  • 🚫 No account required. No telemetry. No analytics. No tracking of any kind.
  • 🏢 Built for regulated industries, NDAs, and anyone who needs meeting content to stay inside the building.
