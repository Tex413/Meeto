## Short answer
Select **Mute voice** in the bottom-right corner. Speech stops at once, and nothing is spoken until you unmute. The button also works in presenter view.

## Steps
1. Select **Mute voice**. It changes to **Voice muted · Unmute**.
2. Read answers on their **board member** cards. Muting does not stop the microphone, transcription, cards or AI requests.
3. To stop the microphone too, select **Stop** on the toolbar.
4. To allow speech again, select **Voice muted · Unmute**. Only answers requested from then on are spoken; one that was being prepared when you muted is not played.
5. Check the button before a meeting: your browser remembers the setting after a reload.
