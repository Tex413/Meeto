## Short answer
In the desktop app, use **Generate transcript** in the meeting's **Recording** tab. It **replaces the saved transcript**.

## Steps
1. If you want to keep the current transcript, open the **Transcript** tab and copy it first.
2. Check **Context**; its names and terms are used for corrections.
3. In the **Recording** tab, select **Generate transcript**.
4. Keep this meeting open until the progress shows **Done**. Opening another meeting stops it.
5. Review the **Transcript** tab. If parts failed, it can be incomplete or empty.
