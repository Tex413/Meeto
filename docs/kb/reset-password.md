## Short answer
Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and use the emailed link within 1 hour. If no email arrives, ask your administrator.

## Steps
1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**. The same confirmation appears whether or not the email is registered.
3. Open the link in the email.
4. Under **Set a new password**, enter at least 6 characters twice and select **Set new password**.
5. **Reset link is invalid or has expired** means you need to request a new link.
6. If no email arrives, check your spam folder and contact your administrator. Some servers do not have email set up.
