## Short answer
With Anthropic selected, the app web-searches company names heard during a meeting, in both injection modes. **Suggest & approve** also offers to speak what it found.

## Steps
1. In **Settings**, select **Anthropic (Claude)** with a saved key. Research does not run with OpenAI.
2. Choose **Injection mode**: **Ask-only** keeps research quietly for your questions; **Suggest & approve** shows a banner. Select **Save**.
3. Get consent, select **Record**, then **Start listening**. Research needs both.
4. In the banner, select **Speak** to hear the summary and add a card. While the voice is muted, it only adds the card.
5. Select **Dismiss** to keep it silent. The summary is still added as a card.
6. Verify the summary; it does not reliably show the pages it came from. To prevent these searches, do not use the Anthropic provider.
