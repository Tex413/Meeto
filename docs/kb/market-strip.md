## Short answer
The strip shows WTI Crude, Brent Crude, Henry Hub, NGL Conway and energy headlines; a badge shows BKV stock. These are indications, not live trading data, and **NGL Conway is an estimate derived from WTI**.

## Steps
1. Read the strip above the transcription controls. Henry Hub is the published figure adjusted by the app.
2. Point at headlines to pause them; select a linked headline to open it.
3. Select **Refresh market prices** to update prices. It does not refresh headlines.
4. Optionally save your **EIA.gov API Key** in **Settings**; it is used when you refresh. Without it, a rate-limited demo key is used.
5. **Market prices unavailable**, **No energy headlines available** or **BKV --** mean that data could not be fetched. Other features still work.
6. The symbols cannot be changed on screen.
