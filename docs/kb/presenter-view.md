## Short answer
Select the toolbar orb to show a large status orb. It is a status light, not an avatar, and does not tell people what is captured.

## Steps
1. Select the orb at the right of the toolbar.
2. Read the label: **Idle**, **Listening…**, **Thinking…** or **Speaking…**.
3. Select **Exit presenter view**, click anywhere else, or press Esc to return. **Mute voice** stays available and does not close the view.
4. Tell participants yourself that an AI is present and what it captures.
