## Short answer
The room camera is turned off in this version while a legal review is pending, and the **Enable room camera** button is hidden. No setting in the app turns it on.

## Steps
1. Do not look for the camera button; it is not shown.
2. Know what the feature does where a developer runs it for testing: it estimates the number of faces and detects a raised hand, does not identify people, and does not save or send camera images.
3. Even then, it opens the camera only after the [consent step](meeting-consent.md).
