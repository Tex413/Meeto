## Short answer
**Record A/V**, available only in the desktop app, saves the primary screen with microphone and system audio (where available) to the meeting. It is separate from transcription.

## Steps
1. Gather everyone who will be heard or seen, and clear anything private from your primary screen.
2. Select **Record A/V** and complete the [consent step](meeting-consent.md); the announcement and answers become part of the recording. A meeting is created if none is active.
3. For a live transcript too, select **Start listening**.
4. Select **Stop recording**, or **End**, and wait while **Saving…** is shown. Do not close the app before saving finishes.
5. Watch it in **History**, in the meeting's **Recording** tab.
6. Recording again in the same meeting adds a new part; earlier parts are kept. Choose a part in the **Recording** tab.
7. The default microphone is recorded, not necessarily the one in the microphone menu. If it cannot open, recording continues without it.
