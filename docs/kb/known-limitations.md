## Short answer
There is no consent step, local AI backend, reliable source citation, typed question box, speaker identification or cross-meeting memory.

## Steps
1. Get consent yourself before **Start listening**, **Record A/V** or the room camera.
2. Choose only **OpenAI** or **Anthropic (Claude)**. LM Studio, Claude Code and Codex are not available, and meeting text can leave your network.
3. Verify answers and cards; they do not reliably name sources. Questions are by voice only.
4. Do not expect **Others only** to keep your words out of cards, or any speaker identification.
5. Use the app for cards and action items. It does not create plans, documents, slides or code changes, and only uploaded documents serve as organisational data. No bot joins Zoom, Teams or Meet.
6. Select **Clear all** between meetings, after **End** and before **Record**; no project memory carries over.
7. Export to-dos before leaving the page. History and CSV do not include them, CSV includes deleted cards, and AI transcript cleanup does not change the saved transcript.
8. **Record A/V** and **Generate transcript** need the desktop app; **Web Speech** needs Chrome. The orb is not an avatar.
