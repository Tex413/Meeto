## Short answer
The current build lacks several capabilities in the product charter; plan meetings around the controls actually shown.

## Steps
1. In **Settings**, choose only the implemented OpenAI or Anthropic provider. LM Studio, Claude Code, and Codex are not selectable here.
2. Obtain consent outside the app before capture; **Record**, **Start listening**, and **Record A/V** have no enforced participant-consent step.
3. Do not rely on all factual cards having source citations; the current card flow does not enforce them.
4. Use **Record A/V** only in the desktop app and **Web Speech** only in Chrome.
