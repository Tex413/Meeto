## Short answer
There is no local AI backend, reliable source citation, typed question box, speaker identification or cross-meeting memory. The consent step uses draft wording that no lawyer has approved, and the room camera is turned off.

## Steps
1. Do not treat the consent step as proof of consent or of meeting any law; it records the host's statement. Ask a lawyer what your meetings require.
2. Choose only **OpenAI** or **Anthropic (Claude)**. LM Studio, Claude Code and Codex are not available, and meeting text can leave your network.
3. Verify answers and cards; they do not reliably name sources. Questions are by voice only.
4. Do not expect **Others only** to keep your words out of cards, or any speaker identification.
5. Use the app for cards and action items. It does not create plans, documents, slides or code changes, and only uploaded documents serve as organisational data. No bot joins Zoom, Teams or Meet.
6. No project memory carries over. After **End**, starting the next meeting on the same page clears the live transcript, cards and to-dos.
7. Export to-dos before leaving the page or starting the next meeting. History and CSV do not include them, CSV includes deleted cards, and AI transcript cleanup does not change the saved transcript.
8. **Record A/V** and **Generate transcript** need the desktop app; **Web Speech** needs Chrome. The orb is not an avatar.
