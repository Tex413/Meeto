## Short answer
**Invalid license key** or **Invalid or unrecognized license key.** means the app could not verify the text you pasted.

## Steps
1. Copy the whole key again. It begins with `MEETINTEL2-`.
2. Make sure you did not copy extra characters or lose any part of it.
3. Paste it into the trial dialog (web) or **License key** (desktop) and select **Activate**.
4. If it is still rejected, contact your administrator, who can check it in the admin console. Do not put the key in a support message.
