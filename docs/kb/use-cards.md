## Short answer
Pin, delete, filter or auto-clear cards, and set how often and how fully they are written. Verify facts yourself; cards do not reliably name sources.

## Steps
1. Set **Interval** (10 to 120 seconds, default 20) and **Terseness** (**terse**, **balanced** or **detailed**).
2. Select a card's **Pin** button to keep it at the top; select it again to unpin.
3. Select a card's **Delete** button to remove it from the live list and History. It can still appear in the CSV export.
4. Type in **Filter cards…** to search; use the clear button to reset.
5. Choose an **Auto-clear** time (5 to 60 minutes) to fade unpinned cards from the live list. They stay in History.
6. Check **To-dos**: cards tagged as action items add their titles there.
7. Treat **$… est.** as a rough card-analysis figure at one fixed rate, not your bill.
