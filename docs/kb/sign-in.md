## Short answer
In the web app, use the **Create account** or **Sign in** tab; sign out with the lock icon. The desktop app has no sign-in.

## Steps
1. To create an account, select **Create account**, enter your email and a password of at least 6 characters twice, and select **Create account & start trial**.
2. To sign in, select **Sign in**, enter your email and password, and select **Sign in**.
3. **Email already registered** means you should sign in or reset the password. **Incorrect email or password** means one of the two is wrong.
4. To sign out, select **Sign out** (lock icon) on the toolbar.
5. A sign-in lasts no more than 8 hours in the browser, and a server restart signs everyone out. If the app stops saving or loading, reload the page and sign in again.
