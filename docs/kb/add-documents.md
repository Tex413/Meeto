## Short answer
Add a supported file under **Documents**. Related passages are then used for cards and spoken answers, and are sent to your AI provider.

## Steps
1. Under **Documents**, select **Add**.
2. Choose a `.txt`, `.md`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.html` or `.htm` file no larger than 20 MB.
3. Wait for indexing; the first upload can take a minute or more.
4. Confirm the file appears with its size and chunk count.
5. To remove it, point at it, select **Remove** and confirm.
6. There is no document search box or viewer, and answers do not reliably name the document used.
